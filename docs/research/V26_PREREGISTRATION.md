# v26: the squeeze shield for the small-cap shorts: pre-registration

Written and committed on 2026-10-21, before any v26 result was computed, at the owner's request ("prepara un run
nocturno ... piensa todo lo que te gustaria probar"; plan approved with "bien"; selection by Sortino alone, without a
tail rule: "sin regla"). Exact rules: `research/v26_shield.py`, `scripts/run_v26.py` (on `scripts/run_v22.py`), the
data in `scripts/run_v26_data.py`; tests in `tests/test_v26.py`.

## Why

Every family that passed in v14-v25 is a short of small caps that gapped up or ran, mostly held to the third close
(V19-V25 results). Its limit is the tail: single trades losing 100-325% of the position when a squeeze gaps through the
stop overnight, which caps the size the owner's ruin rule allows (V19S, V20S). v26 does not look for a new entry. It
keeps the entries that already passed and tests exits that may cut the tail: holding periods, stops, stops that stay
live in the after-hours session and the next premarket, an exit when the price rallies in those sessions, split covers
and name filters, all under R4 costs with measured extended-session spreads.

## 1. Data

- **New: the v26 night store** (`scripts/run_v26_data.py`, Actions cache `v26-store-*`): Alpaca SIP one-minute bars
  (raw prices) from 16:00 ET of a session to 09:29 ET of the next one, for every small cap with an event on that
  session or one of the five before it. An event: a gap of 15% or more, a high 40% or more over the prior close, a close
  30% or more over it, a prior day of +50% or more, or a close 50% or more over the lowest close of the 5 or 20 sessions
  before; prior close $1-$20, prior 20-day dollar volume >= $500k; at most 150 events a session. Nights are fetched in
  a seeded random order within a 130-minute budget; a night that was not fetched has no extended-session rule (the
  trade is walked on the daily bars only), and the report states the coverage. Alpaca's SIP has no bars between 20:00
  and 04:00, so that window is not modelled.
- **New: extended-session spreads**: Alpaca SIP quotes in 3,000 minutes drawn from the night store (seeded; 375 per
  price tier and session): half the median quoted spread over the mid per tier is the cost of an after-hours or
  premarket leg; with fewer than 50 quotes in a cell, twice the session's half-spread of that tier.
- The v18b inputs (daily bars, the v14 event store, SEC shares outstanding and offering filings, the borrow table),
  the v23 liquid store and the v24 mover store, as in v23-v25.

## 2. Entries (fixed, from the source runs)

The 23 families that passed their gate: v19r XS1, XS2, DS1, ON1; v20 GF, RF, RU, NS; v22c IV, IR, IF; v23b SR, VW, EM,
OS, MD, SQ, PT, OR; v25 AS, MP, IS, PS. Their entries are the distinct walk-forward picks of each family
(docs/research/data/*_summary.json; 103 entries), generated by each family's own code (configuration numbers exactly as
the source run numbered them). An intraday entry is generated with its exit set to d3 (the entry, its stop and its
priority do not depend on the exit); a daily entry is the family's own specification (at the open or the close of the
entry row). v22's daily families RS and GP are left out: their exits are rule masks of v22's own daily book, not a
holding period. Every entry is a short; a long entry stops the run with an error.

## 3. The shield (crossed with every entry)

- **Hold** d1, d2, d3, d5: out at the close of the first, second, third or fifth session counting the entry session
  (an entry at a close: counting the sessions after it).
- **Stop** own (the family's own stop; a daily family without a stop has none), 10%, 20%, 35%, 50% over the entry,
  2 or 4 times the 14-day average true range of the sessions before (20% where it is unknown). Live intraday on the
  entry day's minute bars after the entry and on the daily highs after it (filled at the open when the open is beyond
  it, else at the stop slipped a quarter of the way to the bar's high), as in v19.
- **Extended-session stop** none, after-hours, premarket, both: the stop is also live on the night store's bars
  (16:00-19:59 of a held session; 04:00-09:29 of the next), filled at the bar's open when it opens beyond the stop, else
  slipped as above; an after-hours exit pays no borrow for that night.
- **Rally exit** none, +5%, +15%: out in the extended sessions at the close of the first bar 5% (15%) over the last
  regular close.
- **Split cover** all at the end; half at the first close; a third at each of the first two closes.
- **Name filter** none; shares outstanding known and at least 10 million; at least 20 million; an offering filing
  (424B, S-1, S-3, F-1, F-3) in the 63 sessions up to the entry; no +100% day in the 60 sessions before the session
  before the entry. The FINRA short-volume filter of the plan is dropped: that data is not in the prepared inputs.
- An intraday entry held to its own close (d1) has no night, so no extended-session rule and no split cover.

427,525 configurations: 19 intraday families x their entries x 3,815, 4 daily families x their entries x 5,040. With
hold d3, own stop and nothing else, the shield is exactly v19's d3 exit and v19's daily path (tested).

Costs: R4 (v21) with two more legs, the after-hours and the premarket (section 1); a split exit is charged the
half-spread of its last leg; borrow is charged on the part held each night. Scenarios low, middle, high (spreads x2,
borrow x3), auction and gross, as in v22-v25. Sizing as in the source runs (intraday 0.5% risk to the stop, 20% cap,
2% of the dollar volume before the entry, five trades a day; daily 5% cap, 1% of the 20-day dollar volume).

## 4. Selection (run_v22's procedure, unchanged)

Walk-forward 2018-2026 with the activity rule (at least 50 trades and a trade in 40% of the months before), plateau
picks by family each year on earlier data, 500 placebos for each selected configuration (each real trade moved to a
random candidate of its family's scope and band that day, walked under the same shield and name filter), the gate,
`COMBO26` (a family joins a year when its pick scored at least 1.0 and beat its placebos at p <= 0.05; inverse-volatility
weights on earlier data), CPCV PBO, Hansen's SPA and the deflated Sharpe over all v26 configurations and the earlier
trials. Target: COMBO26 monthly Sortino 4 (3 in each half, PBO under 20%, 3 in 2023-07+). Selection by Sortino alone
(the owner); the tail rule (a configuration may be picked only if no earlier trade lost more than 100% of the position)
is reported for comparison only.

## 5. After the report (stated in advance)

- **Own exits**: each family's source walk-forward stream (its own picks and exits) at v26's costs, against its v26
  stream; for v22c, v23b and v25 it must reproduce the source run (same costs).
- **Robustness battery** of each family's v26 walk-forward stream: entry 1 and 5 minutes later (intraday); slippage
  x2; spreads x1.25, x1.5, x2 and the multiple (scan 1-8 by 0.5) at which the stream's CAGR falls to zero; borrow fees
  x2, x3, x5; no short at all where the short-sale restriction is in force (a 10% fall that session before the entry or
  the session before); without the best 1% and 5% of its trades; without its best year; 20%, 35% and 50% of the trades
  dropped at random (50 draws each: shares not borrowable).
- **One account**: COMBO26's members each year (A: their v26 picks; B: the same families and years with their source
  picks and own exits) traded together in one account: one position a symbol at a time across families, at most 20
  open, liquidity caps of v14 on the account; risk 0.25%, 0.5%, 1%, 1.5%, 2%, 3% to the stop (the daily 5% cap scaled
  alike), position caps 20/40/60/100%, gross caps 1/1.5/2/3x, accounts $25k/$100k/$250k, middle and high costs;
  walk-forward statistics and a block bootstrap of 2,000 ten-year paths (mean block 20 sessions). Choice, the owner's
  rule of v19s: the highest median bootstrap CAGR at the middle costs with a chance of a 75% drawdown in ten years of
  at most 1% and gross exposure of at most 2x. A day that loses more than the account ends the book.

## 6. Runs

Data run first (one job). Main run: canary (two configurations of every family, and the after-report stages on a
two-family test summary) -> 300 shards x 4 processes of real returns -> shards that failed are rerun once in the same
run -> selection (only with all 300 shards) -> 300 shards of placebos (failed shards rerun once) -> report -> 23
robustness jobs and 18 one-account jobs -> the final report. Each stage is under GitHub's 6-hour job limit. Market data
stays in the Actions cache; artifacts hold reports and strategy returns.

## 7. Caveats stated in advance

- No window is clean for the researcher: 2018-2026 has been seen through v14-v25. v26 says what is robust; it is not
  a new test that the edge exists.
- Extended-session fills assume a stop-limit order is accepted outside regular hours and filled at the modelled price;
  liquidity in those sessions is thin and the fill size is not capped by the bars' volume. Halts are not modelled
  beyond the bars they leave out.
- Historical borrow availability is not known (a current IBKR snapshot x0.8, plus the random-drop test).
- The bootstrap cannot produce a squeeze bigger than the worst one in the sample.
