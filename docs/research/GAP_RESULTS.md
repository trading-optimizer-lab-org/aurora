# Gap study results: where our returns fall short of Qullamaggie's

GitHub run 39249292175 (2026-10-19), pre-registered in [GAP_PREREGISTRATION.md](GAP_PREREGISTRATION.md). Gate passed:
the 2016-2026 build reproduces the published 2016-2020 base book exactly.

## Summary

- **2020 waterfall** (his self-reported +929%): our base book +170%; with his levers on our own signals (risk 1%,
  cap 35%, 2x margin, whole position trailed on the 20-day average) +613%; with foresight on which trades to keep
  (top 10% of each year) +401% at base size and +3,681% with the levers.
- **Detection is not the gap**: our candidates flagged 72-100% of the monster moves (3x within six months) before
  half the move was done.
- **Slots are a gap**: the base book bought only 14-60% of them (20% in 2020, 14% in 2021); it was already full.
- **Exits are the biggest rules gap**: when bought, the median share of the move captured was 0-21%. Even a book
  that only buys monster stocks (with foresight) made just +30% in 2020 at base size: tight stops at the day's low,
  half sold on day 3 and the 20-day trail shake us out of the big trends.
- **His levers cost a near-ruinous drawdown**: the configurations that make +400-600% in 2020 lose 61-95% at some
  point in 2021-2026 (he reports losing more than half in 2022). The best balance in the grid,
  `MIX_XQ3_20_R1_C20_G2_P0` (risk 1%, 2x margin), made 64% a year in 2016-2020 (Sortino 4.79, drawdown -31%) and 54%
  a year in 2021-2026 (Sortino 3.03, drawdown -61%); picking it after seeing 2021-2026 is hindsight.
- What remains is selection: discretionary choice of the few winners, his early small-account trades in illiquid
  names our filters exclude, and the survivorship of famous traders.
