# Contradictions, negative evidence and expected failure modes

| Question | Evidence/mechanism favoring one sign | Evidence/mechanism favoring the opposite sign | V2 decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overnight move | Information and inventory effects can persist. | Opening imbalances can reverse intraday or overnight. | Freeze continuation and reversal candidates separately. |
| High downside variation | Bad volatility can persist and precede stress. | Extreme downside risk can create rebound premia. | Use signed balance and robust reversal as different families. |
| High volume move | Informed trading can continue. | Risk-sharing or attention shocks can reverse later. | Use signed pressure without claiming a universal sign. |
| High dispersion | Fragmentation may signal deterioration. | Rotation can occur inside an intact bull market. | Continue market direction under low dispersion and reverse under high dispersion as one explicit hypothesis only. |
| Cyclical leadership | Can indicate risk appetite. | Systematic sector rotation has weak/negative evidence in some studies. | Keep ratios simple, label negative evidence and require full multiple-testing correction. |
| Near trailing high | Anchoring and momentum can support continuation. | Aggregate indexes can mean-revert after crowded highs. | Test only frozen cutoffs; no post-hoc sign reversal. |
| Low variance ratio / negative autocorrelation | Supports reversal. | Estimates are noisy and regime dependent. | Use the estimated sign mechanically; report instability. |
| Shallow tree | Can capture a few nonlinear interactions. | Flexible selection can overfit even at shallow depth. | Fully freeze features, refit, depth, leaf size, seed and cumulative trial count. |
